# UI/UX Design Brief — Visual & Interaction Design Guide

**So the AI doesn't just build something functional — it builds something you'd actually use.**

---

## Aesthetic

- **Direction:** Clean, reverent, gentle — inspired by prayer apps and calm digital experiences
- **Mood:** Warm, trustworthy, peaceful — not cold/corporate, not playful
- **Reference:** Calm apps like Headspace, religious imagery sites, soft editorial design

---

## Primary Color

- **Color:** `#4A6FA5` (Muted steel blue — trustworthy, calm)
- **Usage:** Primary buttons, links, active states

---

## Background Color

- **Light Mode:** `#FAFAF8` (Warm off-white — gentle on eyes)
- **Dark Mode:** `#1A1A2E` (Deep navy — reverent, focused)

---

## Text Color

- **Primary:** `#2D3748` (Dark slate — readable, not harsh black)
- **Secondary:** `#718096` (Muted gray — less important text)
- **On Dark Background:** `#E2E8F0`

---

## Accent / CTA Color

- **Color:** `#C9A227` (Muted gold — warmth, divine light)
- **Usage:** Call-to-action buttons, highlights, important indicators

---

## Font

- **UI Font:** Inter — clean, modern, highly readable
- **Heading Font:** Merriweather — serif, traditional, trustworthy
- **Fallbacks:** system-ui, Georgia, serif

---

## Border Radius

- **Cards:** 12px rounded corners
- **Buttons:** 8px rounded corners
- **Input fields:** 6px rounded corners
- **Images:** 8px rounded corners

---

## Shadows

- **Cards:** Subtle `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` — lifted, not heavy
- **Modals:** `0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)` — clearly elevated
- **No harsh drop shadows**

---

## Dark/Light Mode

- **Default:** Light mode primary
- **Toggle:** User can switch to dark mode via icon in navbar
- **Preference:** Stored in localStorage for persistence

---

## Reference Apps

- Headspace (calm, clean interface)
- Bible.com (functional, reverent)
- Calm app (soft, peaceful aesthetic)

---

## Mobile

- **Fully responsive** — chat interface adapts to all screen sizes
- **Chat input:** Sticky at bottom on mobile
- **Message bubbles:** Full width on mobile (more touch-friendly)
- **No bottom tab nav** — top navbar is sufficient

---

## Accessibility

- **Contrast ratios:** WCAG AA compliant (4.5:1 for text)
- **Font sizes:** Minimum 14px for body text
- **Touch targets:** Minimum 44x44px for buttons
- **Focus indicators:** Visible focus rings on keyboard navigation
