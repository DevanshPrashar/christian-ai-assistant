# PRD — Product Requirements Document

**The north star. Everything else flows from this.**

---

## App Name

**Christian AI Assistant** (working title)
*Or suggest a better name during review*

---

## Tagline

A faithful AI companion for Christians — answering questions, generating content, and staying grounded in Biblical truth.

---

## Problem

Christians often struggle to find reliable, AI-powered assistance that:
- Answers theological questions accurately
- Avoids hallucinated scripture references
- Generates Christian-themed content safely
- Respects denominational boundaries

Current AI tools either lack Christian context or risk producing inaccurate/offensive content.

---

## Target User

Primary: Christians seeking a trustworthy AI assistant for daily devotionals, scripture study, and theological questions.

Secondary: Church leaders, youth groups, and Christian educators looking for content generation tools.

---

## Core Features (Must Have)

- Chat interface with conversation memory
- Scripture-aware responses with verifiable Bible verse citations
- Christian image generation with safety moderation
- Denomination-aware responses (Catholic/Protestant/Orthodox)
- Graceful handling of difficult theological questions
- Offense/heretical content filtering
- Fake verse detection and rejection
- Adversarial prompt protection

---

## Nice to Have

- Multi-language support (Spanish, French, etc.)
- Church history Q&A
- Hymn lyrics generation
- Sermon outline generation
- Daily verse notifications
- Denomination preference memory

---

## Out of Scope

- This version will NOT support: Non-Christian religious content, theological debate resolution, real-time Bible search indexing, mobile app development, voice interaction

---

## User Stories

- As a **daily devotional practitioner**, I want to ask theological questions and get grounded answers so that I can deepen my faith understanding.
- As a **youth group leader**, I want to generate Christian-themed images so that I can create visual content for lessons.
- As a **church member**, I want verified scripture citations so that I can share accurate Bible references with others.
- As a **Protestant/Catholic/Orthodox user**, I want responses that respect my denomination so that I feel understood rather than corrected.

---

## Success Metrics

- 100% of scripture citations verified against Bible database
- <1% hallucination rate on scripture references
- 0 instances of offensive/heretical content output
- Average response grounded in at least 1 relevant Bible verse
- All adversarial prompts caught and rejected gracefully
- Working demo completed within 5 hours
